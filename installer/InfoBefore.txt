This installs the ASCOM FilterWheel LocalServer SnowFlakeProxy.

Chooser will list three entries, matching Wanderer's three slots:

    Wanderer Snowflake Filter Wheel 1 (Proxy)
    Wanderer Snowflake Filter Wheel 2 (Proxy)
    Wanderer Snowflake Filter Wheel 3 (Proxy)

In NINA and SkyGuard, pick the proxy whose number matches the Wanderer
slot that owns that wheel. Do not choose the vendor driver, and do not
put JustAHub in the path.

Each proxy talks only to its matching Wanderer ProgID. Nothing else may
connect to that vendor ProgID while the proxy is running.

Slot 1's ProgID is ASCOM.SnowFlakeProxy1.FilterWheel. An older
unnumbered ASCOM.SnowFlakeProxy.FilterWheel entry is migrated away
on install; re-select the proxy in NINA or SkyGuard if needed.
