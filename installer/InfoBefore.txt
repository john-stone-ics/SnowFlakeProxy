This installs the ASCOM FilterWheel LocalServer:

    Wanderer Snowflake Filter Wheel 1 (Proxy)

After setup, choose that name in NINA and SkyGuard. Do not choose the
vendor driver "Wanderer Snowflake Filter Wheel 1", and do not put
JustAHub in the path.

The proxy talks to the installed Wanderer driver. The Snowflake must be
attached to this PC, and nothing else may connect to the vendor ProgID
while the proxy is running.
